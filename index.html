ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Bringing Dental Care Closer to Everyone.

BNU Dental Care connects patients who need dental care with dental students looking for clinical cases to fulfill their academic requirements in a more organized way.

One Connected Experience

Bring case publishing, student requests, patient approval, and private communication together in one organized platform.

01

Patient First

Patients can publish their dental concerns, review student requests, and choose who will be assigned to their case.

Dental Student Workspace

How BNU Dental Care Works.

A clear process connecting patients with dental students.

01

Create an Account

Create a patient account or a dental student account.

02

Publish or Discover Cases

Patients publish cases, and dental students explore the available public cases.

03

Request & Get Accepted

Students send requests. The patient reviews them and accepts one student for the case.

04

Start Private Communication

After acceptance, the assigned student and patient can communicate through private chat.
